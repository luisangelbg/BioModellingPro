AFile formats

The app does not convert files or upload them to any server: it reads them on your computer. This is the complete list of what it recognises and of how each thing has to be named.

A.1 · Your own records (Block 1)

AspectWhat it accepts
Format.csv, .txt (separated by commas, semicolons or tabs), .xlsx, or text pasted from a spreadsheet.
Minimum columnsSpecies, longitude and latitude. Headers are recognised in Spanish and in English (especie/species, longitud/lon/longitude, latitud/lat/latitude) and, if they do not match, they are assigned by hand with the three menus.
Optional columnsCountry, state, locality, year, month, elevation and uncertainty in metres. They are recognised automatically and used in the filters of Block 2.
CoordinatesDecimal degrees with a point or a comma; degrees-minutes-seconds with symbols or with compass letters. Two checkboxes fix in one go swapped longitude and latitude and a missing minus sign.
EncodingUTF-8 and the old Windows encoding: accents are repaired on reading.

A.2 · Raster layers (Blocks 5, 9 and 10)

LayerExpected file name and notes
Bioclimaticwc2.1_<res>_bio_1.tif … bio_19.tif, where <res> is 30s, 2.5m, 5m or 10m. GeoTIFF in geographic coordinates (EPSG:4326).
Elevationwc2.1_<res>_elev.tif
Köppen-GeigerA categorical GeoTIFF with the class codes; the table of names is included in the app.
Monthlywc2.1_<res>_tmin_01.tif … _12.tif, and the same for tmax and prec: 36 files. A twelve-band file per variable is also accepted.
Future scenariosA 19-band file with the bioclimatic variables of the scenario, or separate bio_N layers. They must have the same resolution and extent as those of the present.
Your own regionsGeoJSON of polygons in EPSG:4326, with a recognisable name in the properties of each polygon.

BFormulas of the indices

The same ones shown in the help cards of the app, with the source they come from. The symbols follow the notation of each original source.

Correlation and variable selection (Block 6)
Pearson correlationr = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) / √[Σ(x−x̄)² · Σ(y−ȳ)²]
Coefficient of determinationr² = r × r
Variance inflation factorVIF_j = 1 / (1 − R²_j)
Descriptive statistics (Block 7)
Standard deviationSD = √[ Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1) ]
Coefficient of variationCV % = 100 × SD / |mean|
Multivariate analysis, ecology and diversity (Block 8)
Contribution to the dimensionreference = 100 / (number of variables)
Inertia (correspondence analysis)total inertia = χ² / n
Nearest neighbourR = mean observed distance / mean distance expected at random
Levins niche breadthB = 1 / Σp² ; standardised B = (B − 1) / (classes − 1)
Schoener overlapD = 1 − ½ Σ |p_A,i − p_B,i|
ShannonH′ = − Σ p_i · ln(p_i)
SimpsonD = Σ p_i²
Pielou evennessJ′ = H′ / log(S)
Berger–Parker dominanced = N_max / N
Margalef and Menhinick richnessD_Mg = (S − 1) / ln(N) ; D_Mn = S / √N
Chao1Chao1 = S + f₁² / (2·f₂)
Modelling (Block 9)
TSSTSS = sensitivity + specificity − 1
Omissionomission = presences below the threshold / test presences
Agroclimate (Block 10)
Growing degree daysGDD = Σ max(0, (T_max + T_min)/2 − T_base)
Reference evapotranspirationET₀ = 0.0023 · R_a · (T_mean + 17.8) · √(T_max − T_min)
Aridity indexAI = annual precipitation / annual ET₀

Besides the formula, each help card in the app gives the reading scale, the most common mistake and the full reference. There are 97 cards spread across Blocks 6 to 10.

DMessages and troubleshooting

The warnings the app can show, with their cause and their remedy. Each chapter also has its own table of common problems.

MessageWhat it means and what to do
“Could not query GBIF”No connection, or the service is down. Use the bundled example or your own files and try again later.
“the connection to GBIF was lost”The download broke off after six attempts. The app offers to use the records that did arrive.
“GBIF answered 429 / 503”Too many requests, or the service is overloaded. The app waits and retries by itself; if it keeps happening, try again in a few minutes.
“Extract the raster values first”Block 6 needs the points × variables table from Block 5.
“none recognised as WorldClim or Köppen”The file names do not follow the pattern of appendix A.2.
“The prepared grid uses the extent…”An informative notice: it says which box the model was prepared with. If you changed the extent afterwards, prepare again.
“N of M records fall outside the extent”Those records fall outside the chosen box and do not enter the model. It is your decision, not an error.
“Few presences (<15)”The model will be unstable. Enlarge the area, use higher-resolution layers or switch to an envelope method.
“No monthly layers: the indices will be derived from … bioclimatic layers”There are no monthly layers: the agroclimatic indices come from a reconstructed cycle and are flagged as approximate.
“The pH range … is reported but not applied”There is no soil-pH layer, so that requirement of the crop is left out of the calculation.
“Error reading the GeoTIFF files”A damaged file, or one in a projection other than EPSG:4326.
“Preparing the Python engine…” takes a whileIt is the first time in the session: Python is being downloaded into the browser. After that it stays in memory.

iCite the data, not just the program

A result rests on three things, and all three are cited: the program, the data you worked with and the method you applied. If you download your records from gbif.org instead of from the app, GBIF gives you a download DOI that identifies exactly that dataset: use it.
